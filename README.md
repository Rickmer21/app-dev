# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad 🧪

> *"I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger."* — **Walter White**

**Breaking Bad** is a critically acclaimed American crime drama television series created by **Vince Gilligan** and set in **Albuquerque, New Mexico**. The story follows **Walter White**, an underpaid high school chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing and selling methamphetamine after being diagnosed with inoperable lung cancer to secure his family's financial future.

---

## Why It Stands Out
* **Character Transformation:** Watches Walter White evolve from a mild-mannered teacher into the ruthless kingpin *Heisenberg*.
* **Tightly Written Arcs:** Every season builds naturally toward high-stakes payoffs with minimal filler.
* **Cinematography & Sound:** Distinctive desert palettes, symbolic color cues, and memorable score work.

---
